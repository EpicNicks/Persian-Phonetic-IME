How letters map

There is no dictionary deciding for you. You spell the letters that appear in the Persian word. Vowels are typed only when they are written, and every a becomes ا. So khub is خوب and shir is شیر.

Digraphs such as sh, kh, ch, gh and zh are recognised as you type. To keep two letters apart, press Enter between them: s Enter h gives س ه, not ش.

When a key has more than one answer

Persian has letters that sound alike. Those keys open a numbered list under the word. Press Tab or ↓ to move through it, or 1 to 9 to pick. The underlined letter is the default if you keep typing.

  • sسصث
  • zزذضظ
  • tتط
  • hهح
  • ghغق
  • a/eاآ

Typing

The whole word is underlined while you type
Space, Enter or punctuation commits it. Backspace edits the Latin letters and Esc cancels.
Persian digits and punctuation
۱۲۳ and ، ؛ ؟ by default. Shift+Space types the zero-width non-joiner, as in می‌خواهم.
Word suggestions, if you want them
An optional bundled dictionary suggests completions, with a profanity filter.
Your settings can't break typing
Change the mapping in a plain TOML file. A mistake in it is skipped, and you get a message with the line number and a fix.

Configurable

The settings window saves each option as soon as you change it, and its look and glass opacity are settings too. Choose what you want from:

  • whether gh types غ or ق, and what a lone c does
  • Persian or Western digits and punctuation
  • what Enter and Space do, and where the list opens
  • optional word suggestions, with learning and typo correction you can turn off

Prefer a file? Edit the plain TOML file at %APPDATA%\PersianPhoneticIme\config.toml to remap any key. A mistake in it is skipped with a message that names the line and suggests a fix, so it never stops you typing.

Install

  1. Download PersianPhoneticIme-0.1.0-x64.msi from the Releases page. Check it against the attached .sha256 file.
  2. Run it. It needs 64-bit Windows 10 or later and asks for administrator approval.
  3. Switch to it with Win+Space, or add it under Settings, Time & language, Language.

Privacy

The input method has no network access and no telemetry. What you type is never logged or stored. This page loads nothing from other sites.
