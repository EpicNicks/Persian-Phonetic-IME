Persian word-frequency data: attribution and licence notice
===========================================================

The autocomplete dictionary (dict.bin) and its source lists in this folder
(fa-wordfreq.tsv, fixture.tsv, eval-prefixes.tsv) are derived from the
Persian word-frequency data of the "wordfreq" project.

wordfreq's Persian frequencies are aggregated from these sources:
  - Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (http://www.wikipedia.org), CC BY-SA.
  - OpenSubtitles, via the OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus
    (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php); used with attribution to
    OpenSubtitles.
  - OSCAR, web text (https://oscar-corpus.com/).
  - Twitter, word statistics only; no Twitter content is stored or republished.

Changes made
  The wordfreq Persian list was filtered and adapted: Unicode NFC normalisation;
  Arabic letter variants mapped to Persian (ي ى to ی, ك to ک, ة to ه); tatweel and
  diacritics removed and their counts merged; tokens containing Latin letters,
  digits, other scripts or symbols removed; zero-width non-joiner (ZWNJ) use
  normalised; bare suffix and prefix tokens and a small list of vulgar words
  removed; frequencies converted to a Zipf scale (log10 of occurrences per
  billion words, two decimals); a small hand-curated list of additional words
  (extra.tsv) added; the result compiled into a compact binary database
  (dict.bin). The build recipe is public: tools/wordlist/ and `xtask build-dict`.
